# BACKFLOW — Boss Design Document
# THE MAW

## Hall of the Drowned — Inscription

> *I take what falls and do not return it.*
>
> *Functionary god of the second flood.*

---

## I. Overview

The Maw is the second of the three surface-sent functionaries. He is the third boss in the critical path. He is the first the player will reasonably lose to more than four times.

He arrives at the second floor of the Pipework with an industrial wet/dry vacuum strapped to his shoulders and a job order taped to his thigh. He is paid by the hour. He does not pray. He does not hate.

Where the Augur drew blood, the Maw takes breath.

---

## II. The Ledger of the Flushed — Entry CXII

> *He came down with the second flood. He listened through a coil and carried his own weather upon his back. He breathed us in and breathed us out, and we were not who we had been when we were returned.*

Entry composed by Witness Calcite of the Order of the Lens. The Order does not record names of the surface-sent. They record what was done.

---

## III. Aesthetic and Silhouette

**Height.** 2.1× Donggyu's height. Larger than the player. Smaller than the Descending God.

**Form.** A long-limbed plumber in a thick canvas coverall. Knee-pads. Work boots. Heavy goggles. The face beneath the goggles is never resolved. Only the suggestion of a jaw, working. A radio is clipped to the collar. A coiled cord runs from it into one ear.

**Vacuum.** An industrial wet/dry vacuum, brass-fittinged, mounted on the back as a pack. It hisses and ticks. A pilot light burns somewhere in the housing. The hose is rigid, jointed at three points, longer than he is tall.

**Silhouette test.** At any frame the Maw reads as a long vertical body with a hunched mass on the back and a horizontal hose extending. The hose is the threat-shape. The player learns to read its angle before its action.

**Animation principle.** He moves like a man who has done this for thirty years. No theatrics. Every gesture is utilitarian. He laughs once in the fight, into the radio. Privately.

---

## IV. The Arena — The Cistern

The Cistern is a square subterranean chamber four screens wide. It sits at the bottom of a low-tier collection junction. Three walls are tile. The fourth is the open mouth of a feeder pipe at upper-right, from which water enters at a controlled rate. The floor is a sloped grating. In normal play it drains as fast as the feeder fills.

The arena's defining property is the waterline. The Maw raises it to do his job.

### Waterline by phase

| Phase | Level | Player effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | At the floor. Dry. | Standard movement. |
| 2 | Mid-knee on Donggyu. | Ground movement reduced 25%. Dash cancelled in water. Aerial movement unaffected. |
| 3 | Full flood, ceiling-deep. | Movement converts to swim. Three pipe-fittings rise from the walls to function as anchor points. |

### Other arena features
- Feeder-pipe stream at upper-right is constant during Phase 2 and Phase 3. Touching it is no damage. It pushes the player into the arena center.
- Pipe-fittings (in Phase 3): left wall low, left wall high, right wall mid, back wall high. They are static. They can be clung to.
- The grating floor remains visible through the water at all phases. The Maw's footprints, where he has walked, leave a small darker trace on the grating. This is decorative. It is also a tell.

## V. Phase 1. THE INTAKE

**HP.** 35 percent of total pool.
**Floor state.** Dry.

The Maw enters from the feeder-pipe at upper-right, drops to the floor, plants himself center-stage, and switches the vacuum to intake mode. A low whine builds in the chamber. The Maw is now stationary or shuffling slowly. The hose end aims at Donggyu.

### Attack 1A. The Draw
*Recurring, every 4 to 6 seconds.*

The hose end emits a directional cone of suction. The cone is visible as a faint distortion. Within the cone, Donggyu is pulled toward the Maw at moderate speed and cannot dash into the cone. He may dash perpendicular to it.

- **Tell.** The vacuum's motor pitch rises a full second before activation. A small puff of dust leaves the hose's intake.
- **Counterplay.** Leave the cone laterally. The Maw cannot pivot quickly. The cone has a 90° arc and a four-screen length.
- **Damage if reached.** One mask. The hose-end strikes the player as he is dragged in.

### Attack 1B. Hose Lash
*Every 8 to 12 seconds.*

The Maw cracks the rigid hose down vertically, sweeping a 60° arc in front of him. Heavy. Telegraphed.

- **Tell.** The Maw plants both feet. He draws the hose back over his shoulder for a full 1.2 seconds before release.
- **Counterplay.** Dash beneath, or pogo on the descending hose. The pogo, when threaded correctly, opens a 0.4-second damage window on the Maw's exposed flank.
- **Damage.** Two masks.

### Attack 1C. Spit Vermin
*Every 10 to 15 seconds.*

The Maw reverses the vacuum flow briefly and expels a single small projectile that arcs toward Donggyu. The projectile is a Flushed, formerly an ally, twisted by intake into a hunched scuttling thing.

- **Tell.** The vacuum motor stalls. It hiccups. Then it reverses. The hose-end coughs.
- **Counterplay.** Kill the projectile in flight (one hit) or evade. If allowed to land, the parasite chases for four seconds, then expires.
- **Damage.** One mask, from the parasite's contact.

### Phase 1 transition
At 65% HP the Maw lowers the hose, turns toward the upper-right feeder pipe, and speaks four words into his radio. The radio answers, distorted. The feeder pipe opens to its second valve. Water enters the arena. The waterline rises to mid-knee over six seconds.

> *Audio cue.* A single low-brass note, held, then bent down a quarter-tone. The vacuum's pitch lowers. The water's pitch rises. They meet.

## VI. Phase 2. THE EXHALATION

**HP.** Middle 35 percent.
**Floor state.** Knee-high water. Ground movement reduced. Dashes cancelled at floor level.

The Maw switches the vacuum to a hybrid wet/dry setting. He exhales more than he draws.

### Attack 2A. Pressure Discharge
*Recurring.*

The hose, instead of drawing, expels a horizontal stream of pressurized water across the arena at the height of the Maw's hip. The stream lasts 1.5 seconds. If hit, Donggyu is pushed the full width of the arena into the opposite wall.

- **Tell.** The vacuum body shudders. The hose stiffens. The pilot light flickers red.
- **Counterplay.** Jump cleanly over (the stream is at hip height) or stand directly behind the Maw (the stream is forward-only).
- **Damage.** Two masks. One additional mask if hit hard against the back wall.

### Attack 2B. Spit Vermin, Multiplied
*Recurring.*

Phase 1's spit returns. In Phase 2 the Maw expels three parasites in a fan rather than one. The parasites float on the waterline and skim toward Donggyu rather than crawling on the floor.

- **Counterplay.** Arc them in flight. Do not let them land in the water near you.

### Attack 2C. Drink
*Every 14 seconds.*

The Maw drives the hose-tip into the waterline. The chamber's water level dips three inches. This appears helpful. It is not.

The Maw then immediately uses Pressure Discharge with the stored water at +50% width.

- **Tell.** The drink is visible (water level dips). The player has two seconds to reposition before the supercharged discharge.
- **Counterplay.** Anticipate. The drink-discharge sequence is the player's best opening. The Maw is committed and slow for 1.2 seconds at the start of the drink animation.

### Phase 2 transition
At 30% HP the radio crackles. A voice, distorted, says one word.

> *finish*

The Maw nods. He turns the feeder-pipe valve all the way. Water rises to the ceiling over four seconds.

> *Audio cue.* Low brass deepens to bass clarinet. Strings enter, dissonant, half-step intervals. The piano motif from the opening cinematic returns, played in reverse.

---

## VII. Phase 3. THE DROWNING

**HP.** Final 30 percent.
**Floor state.** Fully submerged. Donggyu swims. The Maw walks on the floor, the vacuum acting as ballast, exhaling slow bubble-trails. Three pipe-fittings rise from the walls as anchor points.

### Attack 3A. Submarine Pull
*Constant.*

The Maw's intake is now ambient. A continuous undertow toward his position fills the chamber. Donggyu cannot stop swimming.

- **Counterplay.** Position-management. Stay above and behind. The pull is weakest at the player's vertical zenith.

### Attack 3B. The Bell
*Every 8 seconds.*

The Maw raises the hose vertically and discharges upward. A column of water erupts to the ceiling and falls back as five descending waves across the arena, evenly spaced.

- **Tell.** Hose vertical. Vacuum body steamed.
- **Counterplay.** Position on a pipe-fitting. The waves pass through open water but break on the fittings.
- **Damage.** One mask per wave.

### Attack 3C. The Choir of the Drowned
*Once, at 15% HP.*

The Maw discharges the entire contents of his vacuum. Every Flushed ally he has drawn in across his career, spat out at once, in a ring around himself. These are not parasites. They are recognizable.

A Wipe-elder. A Lens-novice. A small grease-mendicant. Each swims toward Donggyu with the slow, apologetic motion of someone who knows they are dying.

Killing them is required to clear the room. Killing them costs the player one Wound of Faith per ally. See Drops, below.

- **Counterplay.** There is no clean counterplay. The Choir must be cleared. The system is asking the player to choose speed over reverence.

---

## VIII. Death

At 0 HP the Maw stops. The vacuum's pitch decays. He drops the hose. He sits, in the waterline, in the dark, like a man on a curb at the end of a shift. The radio crackles. A voice asks for his status.

He does not answer.

The light from the pilot fades. The water begins, slowly, to drain.

The body remains in the arena permanently. Players passing through later will find him in the same position. Head bowed. Untouched by the Flushed. The Flushed do not approach him.

---

## IX. Drops

### The Throat Charm  *(1 notch)*

> *Worn at the throat. The breath comes harder. The world becomes a little harder to swallow.*

**Effect.** Faith gain from defeated enemies increased by 20 percent. Healing speed reduced by 15 percent.

**Acquisition.** Taken from the Maw's body after the fight. The removal uses the same animation as breaking pottery. A small ceremonial gesture.

### Wounds of Faith  *(one per Choir of the Drowned ally killed)*

> *A mark on the soul, in the shape of someone who used to know your name.*

**Effect.** Each Wound permanently advances the run-timer toward the Cleansing by four seconds. The Wounds are visible on Donggyu's mineral armor as small darker streaks on the chestplate. There is no way to remove a Wound.

**Design note.** The player will not learn the cost of these Wounds for several hours of play. The first run will accumulate them freely. Later runs will pause, refuse, or seek alternative routes.

---

## X. Audio Design

- **Pre-fight.** The chamber is silent except for the slow drip of the feeder pipe at upper-right. The drip is in time with the piano motif from the opening cinematic, but slower.
- **Phase 1.** Low strings, sustained. The vacuum's motor is itself a musical element. A sub-bass drone at 60Hz that the score builds upon.
- **Phase 2.** The strings rise a minor third. Low brass enters. The piano motif begins, played on the vacuum's resonant frequency.
- **Phase 3.** The score thins. By the Choir of the Drowned, only solo piano remains, playing the motif at one note per two seconds. Dissonantly resolved.
- **Death.** A single sustained string note that decays for twelve seconds. No resolution.

---

## XI. Ascended Variant  *(Pantheon)*

In the Ascended pantheon, the Maw fights with the following modifications.

- The Choir of the Drowned occurs at every phase transition, not once.
- The Bell discharges seven waves instead of five.
- The Maw's radio voice (his unseen surface supervisor) is audible throughout the fight, narrating his work in a flat unhurried bureaucratic register. Sample lines:

> *Confirmed. Pressure holding. Continue.*
>
> *He is still upright. He is still upright. He is still upright.*
>
> *Confirmed. Confirmed. Almost.*

Pantheon dialogue does not affect gameplay. It exists to disturb.

---

## XII. Notes to the Composer

The Maw's theme is not heroic. The Maw's theme is labor.

The model is not the hero theme. The model is not the duelist's theme. The model is the score for a film about a man in a factory who is about to die. The vacuum is the metronome. The piano motif from the opening cinematic must return in the final phase, played slowly, as if the music itself is tired.

The Maw is paid by the hour. The score should sound paid by the hour.
