# BACKFLOW — Key Art Prompt Set

> *…for those who were flushed.*

## A Note on Voice

These prompts are written first as prose direction for human illustrators or art directors, and second as compressed prompt-strings for image-generation models. The Hollow Knight register is preserved in both. No imagery is to be rendered with contemporary idiom, ironic distance, or any acknowledgment of the premise's absurdity. The Pipework is not a joke. Donggyu is not a meme.

## Tonal Constants

Apply to every piece without exception.

- **Palette**: Lampblack, iron-rust, bone-ivory, mineral pale-green, drain-light gold. Saturation low. Single warm bloom from drain-light when present. No primary blues. No saturated reds.
- **Linework**: Tight pen-and-ink with watercolor wash beneath. Negative space respected.
- **Particulate**: Soap film, hair fibers, paper ash, drifting dust. Always present. The air has weight.
- **Silence**: The viewer should hear no music. Only the slow movement of water in a closed pipe.
- **Composition discipline**: Center the subject or hold the rule of thirds. No dutch angles. No off-kilter framing. The Pipework does not lean.

## I. *The Throne*  (Cover / Title Art)

A frontal, symmetric composition. Donggyu sits upon the porcelain throne, knees together, both hands resting upon the copper pipe laid horizontal across his lap. His eyes, two pale luminous points, are the only living detail. Behind him, drain-light falls from above as a single column of dust-lit gold. It does not reach his face. The throne is cracked. Around its base lie small offerings: a single dissolved lens, a softened scrap of paper, a tied lock of hair. The Flushed are not in frame. He is alone, and he is awaited.

- **Palette**: Lampblack ground, ivory throne, mineral-pale armor, gold drain-light, copper warmth.
- **Lighting**: Single overhead beam, soft falloff. No fill light.
- **Lower frame**: a half-buried tablet, partially legible. The visible inscription reads: *…for those who were flushed.*
- **Title typography**: BACKFLOW in a serifed roman, lightly weathered, set beneath the throne. No subtitle.

**Image-gen prompt:**
> 2D hand-drawn key art in the style of Hollow Knight (Team Cherry, Ari Gibson). A silent masked figure in pale calcified mineral armor sits frontally upon a cracked porcelain toilet rendered as a throne, holding a sharpened length of copper pipe across the lap. Two faint luminous eyes, no other facial features. A single column of dust-lit gold drain-light falls from above. Lampblack background, watercolor wash, ink linework. Small offerings on the ground at the base of the throne: a translucent contact lens, a scrap of softened paper, a coil of dark hair. Devastating quietness. Restrained. Low saturation. Title type at base: BACKFLOW.

## II. *The First Descent*

Vertical composition. Top of frame: a hand on a porcelain handle, pressing. Middle: a small body in a column of water, falling, arms loose. Bottom: dark, with two pale points already gathering below. They are not Donggyu's eyes. They are the eyes of someone watching him arrive. The long fall is suggested by motion blur and the trailing of fiber-particulate. The hand at the top of frame is human. Fingernails, knuckles, a loose wedding ring. We will not see the rest of that body.

- **Palette**: Bleached white-ceramic top, gradient to deep iron-brown center, lampblack base. The water is not blue. The water is gray.
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds inverted. Most weight in the lower black.
- **Diegetic detail**: A second hand entering frame from below, in shadow, palm upturned. Receiving.

**Image-gen prompt:**
> Vertical key art. A human hand at the top presses a porcelain toilet handle. Below, a small unconscious body falls through a column of grey water, arms loose, hair drifting upward. The composition descends through ceramic-white into iron-brown into lampblack. At the bottom, two pale luminous points wait in the dark. Watercolor and ink. Hollow Knight tonal register. Ceremonial.

## III. *The Pipework*  (Establishing Region Card)

A wide-aspect cross-section of a five-story tenement, seen from inside its plumbing. The building is mostly suggested, implied by the geometry of pipes, joists, the occasional cracked wall tile. The pipes are a cathedral. From floor to floor, the system arches and crosses like ribbed vaulting. Small phosphor-soft lights mark Flushed encampments at five distinct strata. A tiny pale figure stands at the lowest stratum, looking up. Distance is the subject.

- **Aspect**: 21:9 panoramic.
- **Architecture cue**: The pipes should suggest a Gothic interior. Naves. Transepts. Clerestory windows of cracked overhead grate. Not a literal building. A cathedral of waste-water.
- **Title type**: THE PIPEWORK, beneath, in the same serifed roman as the cover, smaller.

**Image-gen prompt:**
> Wide panoramic 2D key art. A vertical cross-section of a five-story building shown entirely as its plumbing. Pipes arching like Gothic ribbed vaulting. Five strata stacked vertically, each glowing softly with phosphor Flushed encampments. A single small pale armored figure stands at the lowest stratum looking up. Watercolor wash, ink linework, low saturation. Title beneath: THE PIPEWORK. Hollow Knight register. Cathedral of waste-water.

## IV. *Vespers in the Lensward*

A side-on diorama of a small congregation. Twelve to fifteen Flushed kneel in a half-circle around a shallow basin in which dissolved contact lenses float like cherry blossom petals on still water. The kneeling figures are anthropomorphic but irreducibly *debris*: robed in softened paper, faces partial, postures perfect. One Witness, taller, stands at the basin's far edge, holding a single intact lens between thumb and forefinger, pressed to its own brow. Donggyu stands in shadow at the entrance to the chamber, watching. He has not yet been seen.

- **Lighting**: Drain-light from a small grate in the upper-left, casting one diagonal column across the basin. The lenses scatter it.
- **Palette**: Pale green, ivory, soft warm gold, mineral white.
- **Diegetic detail**: A small inscribed plaque embedded in the floor of the foreground, half visible: *that which is dissolved is at last permitted to see.*

**Image-gen prompt:**
> 2D hand-drawn key art. A small congregation of anthropomorphic debris-creatures, robed in softened paper, kneel in a half-circle around a shallow basin of still water in which translucent contact lenses float. One taller robed figure stands at the basin's far edge holding a single intact lens to its brow. A small pale armored figure watches unnoticed from a shadowed doorway at the side of frame. Drain-light diagonal from upper left scatters across the basin. Hollow Knight tonal register. Watercolor and ink. Reverent silence.

## V. *The Augur*

A two-figure composition along a long horizontal pipe-corridor. Far left, in shadow, the Augur: tall, gaunt, half-silhouette, holds a thin steel probe like a sword raised to draw. The probe's tip catches the only point of warm light. Far right, Donggyu, small, copper pipe held low. Between them, the corridor recedes; the perspective is sharp, predatory. The walls are slick. Above their heads, hairline cracks suggest the surface world beyond. The Augur is not a monster. The Augur is a man at work.

- **Palette**: Mostly grayscale, with a single point of steel-glint and a single point of pale eye-light.
- **Composition**: 60/40 weighting toward the Augur. He claims more frame.
- **Boss title-card type**: THE AUGUR, beneath, smaller still, italicized.

**Image-gen prompt:**
> 2D side-on boss key art. A long horizontal pipe corridor. On the left, a tall gaunt half-silhouetted human plumber raises a thin steel probe; the probe's tip catches a single point of warm light. On the right, a small pale armored figure with two faint luminous eyes holds a sharpened length of copper pipe low. Hollow Knight register. Watercolor, ink, lampblack, single warm highlight. Slick wet walls. The corridor recedes between them. Boss intro title: THE AUGUR.

## VI. *The Maw*

Vertical composition. The frame is half-flooded. The waterline cuts across the middle third. Below: dark, particulate, full of struggling silhouettes. Former Flushed. Now passengers in something else. Above: the Maw, shoulders forward, gripping the wide rigid hose of an industrial wet/dry vacuum that descends into the water like a probe into a wound. The vacuum body is on his back, bulky, brass-fittinged, a small flame visible at a pilot light. His goggles reflect the drain-light. He is not in the frame's center. He is offset to one side, the way a butcher stands at a table. Donggyu is small, mid-water, mid-jump, half-illuminated. The Maw has not yet seen him.

- **Palette**: Above water, sodium-warm and brass-red. Below water, glaucous-green and lampblack. The waterline cuts the palette in two.
- **Composition**: Vertical 3:4. The waterline is the composition's true subject.
- **Title-card**: THE MAW, beneath.

**Image-gen prompt:**
> Vertical 2D boss key art, half-flooded composition with waterline cutting across the middle third. Above the waterline: a hulking plumber in heavy goggles, wearing an industrial wet/dry vacuum strapped to his back, gripping its rigid hose that descends into the water; brass fittings, a small pilot light burning. Sodium-warm tones. Below the waterline: glaucous-green water, particulate, the struggling silhouettes of debris-creature passengers. A small pale armored figure mid-jump, half-emerged from the water, copper pipe in hand. Hollow Knight register. Watercolor and ink. Title beneath: THE MAW.

## VII. *The Descending God*

A vertical composition, taller than wide. The frame is the full five stories of the Pipework, seen from inside, as in piece III. Now a single tube of polished steel descends from the topmost stratum, suspended on cable, slowly. From its tip: light. A single pinpoint of light, brighter than anything else in the game. Beneath it, at four separate strata, the small silhouettes of Donggyu, climbing, climbing, climbing. The Descending God does not look like a god. It looks like a piece of equipment.

- **Palette**: Deep iron-brown ground. The pinpoint of light is the only saturated color. Clinical white, almost cyan at its core.
- **Composition**: Extreme verticality. The eye is forced to travel down the length of the descending tube.

**Image-gen prompt:**
> Extreme vertical 2D key art. Inside a five-story plumbing cathedral, a single polished steel tube descends slowly from the topmost level on a thick cable, its tip emitting one pinpoint of clinical white light. Four separate small frames of a pale armored climbing figure shown at four ascending strata, all in motion upward. Lampblack ground, iron-brown midtones, single cyan-white highlight. Hollow Knight register. No music. Watercolor and ink.

## VIII. *The Quiet Tablet*

A small piece. Macro composition. A single carved brass pipe-fitting, half-buried in calcified mineral residue, the residue forming a soft halo around it. Inscribed on the fitting, legible only if the viewer leans close: *We were not chosen. We were left.* Beside it, a single dissolved lens, almost invisible, almost translucent. Nothing else in frame. No figure.

This is the piece used for end-credits and for loading screens. It is the quietest image in the project.

- **Palette**: Bone-ivory, brass, lampblack, one drop of pale green from the lens.

**Image-gen prompt:**
> Macro still-life key art. A single small brass pipe-fitting half-buried in pale calcified mineral residue, inscribed in carefully carved letters: We were not chosen. We were left. Beside it, a single translucent dissolved contact lens, barely visible. Watercolor and ink, Hollow Knight tonal register. Bone-ivory, brass, lampblack. No figure. No music.

## Appendix — Forbidden Treatments

The following are not permitted in any BACKFLOW key art, in any iteration, for any market:

- Donggyu shown holding the copper pipe in an action pose, mid-strike, with motion lines or impact effects.
- The Flushed rendered with cartoonish eyes, oversized expressions, or fur.
- Plumbing rendered in glossy, clean, photographic detail. The Pipework is *worn*, not modern.
- Any imagery of waste matter beyond the abstract particulate already specified.
- Any imagery referencing or winking at the building's surface. Phones, advertisements, modern signage, contemporary furniture in the bathroom of the prologue.
- Saturated red. Saturated blue. The palette has been chosen. It is not negotiable.
- A grin. Anywhere. By anyone.

The Pipework holds its breath. The art holds it with it.
