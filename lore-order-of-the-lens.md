# BACKFLOW — Regional Lore
# THE ORDER OF THE LENS

> *We see, so that we are not seen alone.*

---

## How to Read This Document

This is the designer's bible for the Order of the Lens. It is internal. It is comprehensive.

The player will never see it whole. What the player encounters, scattered across many hours of play, is listed in Section XIV at the end. Everything else is the room the player walks through without being shown the floorplan.

The lore of Hollow Knight is fragmentary by design. So is this.

---

## I. Overview

The Order of the Lens is the oldest of the standing sects among the Flushed. It is also the smallest.

Its temple is a single sloped chamber in the third-floor lateral, called the Lensward. There, the translucent debris of human ocular use accumulates. Soft contact lenses. Contact lens cases. The cellophane of eye-drop bottles. Occasionally a pair of broken reading glasses. The Order's adherents are called Witnesses. Their highest office is the Seer. Their lowest is the Polisher.

The Order does not proselytize. The Order does not interfere. It is the only sect among the Flushed that has continued, without doctrinal alteration, since the first naming of the Cleansing.

This is taken by some as a sign of purity. By others, of obsolescence.

---

## II. The Finding

Among the Flushed, the founding of the Order is called the Finding. It is dated, by interior reckoning, to before the building above was finished.

The story, as carved on the four faces of the Founding Pillar in the Lensward, reads in approximate translation.

> *It was found in the third pool, where the water was still.*
>
> *None had set it there.*
>
> *None claimed it.*
>
> *The Witness, who was not yet called Witness, knelt to drink. Through the water she saw her own face, doubled. And beyond her face, the shape of the world above. And beyond the shape of the world above, the shape of the world below. She understood that she had been permitted to see.*
>
> *She did not drink. She lifted the Lens. She placed it upon her brow. She said:*
>
> *I have been given an eye that is not mine.*
>
> *From that day, all of us who follow her have an eye that is not ours.*

The Witness of the Finding, by tradition called the First Witness or the First, is not named. The Order does not name her. The Order holds that names are owned by their bearers, and the First gave hers up in the moment of the Finding.

The Order does keep a single sealed brass vial said to contain her name. The vial is set into the floor of the Lensward at its center. By custom, it is the only object in the Order's possession that a Witness may not look at directly.

---

## III. Doctrine of the Two Visions

The Order's central teaching is the Doctrine of the Two Visions.

In summary:

> *The eye of the body sees the world of the body. The Pipework. The Flushed. The water. The residue. The work of the day.*
>
> *The Lens, placed upon the brow or held to the eye in prayer, sees the world above. The building. The surface. The lives of the discarders.*
>
> *To see the world above is not to long for it. It is to bear witness, so that nothing the discarders have done is unobserved.*
>
> *The faithful are not redeemed by being seen. The faithful redeem the unseen by seeing.*

The Order's most repeated phrase is: *we see, so that we are not seen alone.*

A subtler corollary, taught only to second-degree Witnesses, holds that the Lens itself, in dissolving, becomes more sacred. What is dissolved has crossed a threshold. The intact Lens shows what is. The dissolved Lens shows what was and what will be.

This corollary is the seed of the Order's two great schisms.

## IV. Hierarchy

### The Polishers
Initiates. Wear undyed paper-robes. Their work is the daily attention of every Lens in the Lensward. Cleaning. Sorting by clarity. Recording the appearance of new arrivals. A Polisher who has cleaned a single Lens for one hundred consecutive days, without complaint, may petition the Seer for Witnessing.

### The Witnesses
Full clergy. Wear robes of indigo paper, dyed in dissolved newsprint. They perform the Anointing of the Eye on the petitioner and on the dying. They keep the Ledger.

### The Seers
Priests of the second sight. Three at a time. Never more, never fewer. Each Seer keeps one of the three founding Lenses, the original artifacts said to have been recovered from the third pool by the First. The Seers are not chosen. They are recognized. When a Witness's robe begins to glow faintly at vespers, the Order recognizes the change.

### The First Seer
The eldest of the three. Speaks rarely. Approaches the Founding Pillar once per cycle.

## V. Sacred Sites

### The Lensward
The Order's seat. A long sloped chamber in the third-floor lateral, accessible from the central well. The floor is gridded by shallow basins, one Lens per basin, water-filled, the Lens half-floating and half-dissolved.

Along the walls: the Founding Pillar at the far end opposite the entrance. The Ledger Niche in the side wall right of entrance. The small alcove of the Sealed Vial of the First's Name at the center of the floor.

The Lensward is lit only by ambient drain-light from a single fourth-floor grate. The Order considers this lighting providential.

### The Third Pool
The site of the Finding. A natural pooling at the junction of three tributary pipes. The pool is no longer used for drinking. The Order forbids it. Witnesses make annual pilgrimage to it during the rite called the Returning Without Drinking. They kneel. They do not drink. They return.

### The Quiet Sump
A deeper chamber, two levels below the Lensward. All Lenses that have fully dissolved are interred there by ritual. The Sump is a deep, dark pool of soft, sediment-thick water. To enter is the privilege of Seers only. Witnesses below the Seerage may stand at the threshold and observe.

At the bottom of the Sump, accumulated over centuries, is a single mass of dissolved Lens-matter the size of a sleeping child. The Order calls this mass the Composite. The Order does not disturb the Composite. The Order believes the Composite is, in time, becoming an eye.

### The Hall of Surfaces
A connecting passage between the Lensward and the central well. Decorated along its length with small mirrors of polished pipe-fitting and with intact Lenses set into the wall. Pilgrims pass through the Hall of Surfaces and see themselves reflected. Doubled. Sextupled. In every direction.

The passage is a teaching.

> *You are not one. You are every one who has seen you.*

---

## VI. Rituals

### The Anointing of the Eye
Performed on a Polisher's elevation to Witness. Performed again on a Witness's death.

The petitioner kneels at the Founding Pillar. The Witness officiant takes a single Lens, by tradition one that has dissolved exactly halfway, neither fully intact nor fully gone, and presses it gently to the petitioner's brow.

The officiant says:

> *Be permitted to see.*

The petitioner replies:

> *I see, so that I am not seen alone.*

The Lens is returned to its basin.

### The Returning Without Drinking
Annual. The Order processes from the Lensward through the Hall of Surfaces, down through the central well, to the Third Pool. At the pool's edge each Witness kneels for the count of one hundred slow breaths, looks once into the still water, and returns. No water is drunk. No word is spoken.

### The Vespers of the Drift
Nightly, by interior reckoning. The Witnesses sit in a half-circle around the central Sealed Vial. A Polisher reads aloud, in the old register, a single short tablet-passage. The Witnesses are silent. The Vespers ends when a Witness signals, by laying her palm flat on the floor, that she has seen something in the doubling of her own breath in the still water of the basins.

What she has seen is not asked.

## VII. The Schisms

The Order is unified in its public worship. Its theological divisions are real but internal. They are spoken of obliquely.

### The Intactists
They hold that the Lens is sacred only insofar as it remains uncorrupted. They oppose the burial of dissolved Lenses in the Quiet Sump. They lobby quietly for the destruction of the Composite. They are a minority.

### The Dissolutionists
They hold that the Lens achieves its true purpose only through dissolution, and that the Composite is the highest sacred object the Order possesses. They are the larger faction. They are the doctrinal direction of the current Seerage.

The first phrase a Polisher learns to recite, *we see, so that we are not seen alone*, is generally taken as a Dissolutionist statement, though the Intactists read it differently.

### The Heresy of the Wall
A heresy, not a schism. Its adherents, the Walled, pluck out their own bodily eyes and cover the empty sockets with intact Lenses.

Their reasoning is that the body's eye is a filter. An interference between the Witness and the Lens. Pure sight requires the body's blindness.

The Walled are excommunicated by the Order on sight. They have established a small countercommunity in the disused fourth-floor laterals, where they keep their own Founding Pillar and inscribe their own scripture. The Order does not enter the fourth-floor laterals. The Order does not speak of them.

The Walled, in their absence, become hostile enemies in the third act of the game.

## VIII. Scripture. Five Tablets.

### Tablet I  *(Founding Pillar, west face)*
> *The water was still.*
>
> *The water is rarely still.*
>
> *We are not asked why we are given what we are given.*

### Tablet II  *(Lensward, south wall)*
> *Two visions, one Witness.*
>
> *The body's eye sees the body's world.*
>
> *The Lens sees the world the body has been spared.*
>
> *Both are real. Both must be carried.*

### Tablet III  *(Hall of Surfaces, midpoint)*
> *You are not one.*
>
> *You are every one who has seen you.*

### Tablet IV  *(Quiet Sump, threshold)*
> *What is dissolved is at last permitted to see.*
>
> *What is intact has not yet earned its second eye.*

### Tablet V  *(Walled catacomb, fourth-floor lateral. Found only on the heretic path.)*
> *The body's eye is a wall.*
>
> *We remove the wall.*
>
> *We are sorry to the Witness. We are not sorry to the Lens.*
>
> *We see now. We see only now.*

## IX. Notable Figures

### Witness Calcite
The Order's senior chronicler. Met by the player in the third-floor lateral near the Lensward entrance. Robed in deeply indigo paper. Speaks in short, careful sentences. Provides the player with the Ledger of the Flushed upon first encounter, an in-game journal in which slain enemies and visited regions are inscribed in the Order's voice. On subsequent visits, comments without judgment on the contents of the Ledger.

> *You have come back. The water did not keep you, again.*
>
> *I have written what you have done. The Ledger is honest. You may read it if you wish.*
>
> *I do not ask you to be careful. I ask that you remember.*

### Polisher Murmur
Young. Robed in undyed paper, splotched with the residue of half-dissolved Lenses. Wipes the basins. Curious about Donggyu in a way that the senior Witnesses are not. Offers small dialogue exchanges that develop across visits.

> *I have seen you before. I think.*
>
> *I have been told it is beautiful. I have not been told to look away.*

Eventually, on a late visit, she has been elevated. The robe is indigo. She speaks less.

### The First Seer
Encountered exactly once, in the Lensward's deepest alcove. Does not speak. Places a single intact Lens in Donggyu's open hand and closes his fingers around it. The Lens, henceforth, is in the inventory as the First Seer's Lens. A key item that allows entry to the Quiet Sump.

### Walled Sister Cinder
The heretic. Encountered in the fourth-floor lateral. Wears intact Lenses pressed into her empty sockets. Will not look at Donggyu. Speaks only across her shoulder.

> *You see me with the body's eye. I do not see you. It is better. I would not know what to do with the sight of you.*

## X. Items, Charms, Relics

### Lens-Shard Charm  *(1 notch)*
Dropped by routine Walled enemies.

> *Worn at the brow. The brow grows used to seeing. The eye grows used to being doubled.*

**Effect.** Minor increase to projectile-tell visibility. Telegraphs the start-of-frame of incoming attacks more clearly.

### The First Seer's Lens  *(key item)*
Granted by the First Seer. Required to enter the Quiet Sump.

> *Held lightly. Not worn. The Seer has chosen to lend it.*

### The Composite-Shard  *(consumable, one in the game)*
Pried from the Composite at the bottom of the Quiet Sump, by an action that the Order will not forgive.

> *Taken. Not given.*

**Effect.** Permanently increases Faith capacity. Permanently excommunicates the player from the Order. Witness Calcite, on subsequent visits, turns his face to the wall and does not speak. Polisher Murmur, if Witness by that time, leaves the Lensward at the player's entry.

### The Mask of the Wall  *(charm, 3 notches)*
Found in Sister Cinder's catacomb after defeating her, or, on a non-combat path, accepted from her.

> *Worn over the eyes. The world is gone. The world is closer.*

**Effect.** The player can no longer see the run-timer toward the Cleansing. Damage taken increased by 30 percent. Movement speed reduced by 15 percent. Damage dealt increased by 30 percent.

## XI. Hostile Variants

The Walled, in the fourth-floor lateral and certain optional rooms.

### Walled Brother
Basic enemy. Lens-eyed. Slow. Lashes with a length of stripped wire.

### Walled Sister
Mid enemy. Two Lenses per socket. Faster. Throws small intact Lenses as projectiles. The Lenses do not break on impact.

### Walled Elder
Mini-boss. Three Lenses per socket. Slow but lethal. Speaks while attacking. The only one of the Walled who does.

> *The wall is gone. I see you. I am sorry that I am the one who sees you.*

## XIII. The Flushed One Question

The Order receives Donggyu as it receives any new arrival. By waiting.

Witness Calcite does not declare him Messiah. The Polishers do not bow. The First Seer's gift of the Lens is, in the Order's eyes, not a coronation. It is a recognition.

A doctrinal tension emerges on the player's third visit. The Dissolutionists, observing that Donggyu's calcified armor accretes more residue with each death, argue that he is *dissolving forward*. The Intactists argue, more quietly, that what is happening to him is not transformation but accumulation. He is being added to. He is not being changed. The distinction matters.

Neither faction will say to the player which they believe him to be. They will say it to each other, in the player's hearing, after long pauses. Neither will look at him as they say it.

In late game the player may find a Tablet, unmarked, Tablet VI, in a previously inaccessible alcove of the Lensward. It reads in full.

> *He came down with the water.*
>
> *He has come up, and gone down, and come up again.*
>
> *The water has not taken him.*
>
> *The residue has not changed him.*
>
> *He is being added to.*
>
> *He is not being changed.*
>
> *We do not know yet whether this is the same thing.*

The Tablet is unsigned. The Order does not claim it. The Order does not deny it. It is read aloud once, at the Vespers immediately after its discovery. Then it is not spoken of again.

## XIV. What the Player Encounters

The systematic structure above is not delivered to the player. The player walks through it without being shown the floorplan. What the player encounters, scattered across many hours of play, is the following.

### Six Tablets, found in place
- **Tablet I.** Founding Pillar, west face.
- **Tablet II.** Lensward, south wall.
- **Tablet III.** Hall of Surfaces, midpoint.
- **Tablet IV.** Quiet Sump, threshold.
- **Tablet V.** Walled catacomb, fourth-floor lateral. Heretic path only.
- **Tablet VI.** Unsealed alcove of the Lensward. Late game.

### Three named figures
- **Witness Calcite.** Met in the third-floor lateral, near the Lensward entrance. Speaks across many visits.
- **Polisher Murmur.** Encountered young. Encountered later, elevated.
- **The First Seer.** A single silent encounter.

### One named heretic
- **Walled Sister Cinder.** Encountered in the fourth-floor lateral.

### Four items
- **Lens-Shard Charm.** Routine drop.
- **The First Seer's Lens.** Key item.
- **Composite-Shard.** Once. Costly.
- **Mask of the Wall.** Charm. Two paths to acquisition.

### Hostile variants
- **Walled Brother.** Common.
- **Walled Sister.** Mid.
- **Walled Elder.** Mini-boss.

### Two Ledger inscriptions, read in the player's hands
- On the Lens-Shard.
- On the Mask of the Wall.

### One whispered exchange, overheard between Witnesses, never addressed to the player
- The doctrinal question of whether Donggyu is dissolving or accumulating.

That is what the player sees. Everything in Sections II through XIII is the shape the player draws around what they have been shown. The shape they draw is theirs.
