# BACKFLOW — Opening Cinematic Script

> Running time: **~75 seconds**
> Shot count: **22**
> Spoken words: **zero**

## Production Notes

The cinematic plays at 24 fps. The score is one solo piano motif. Three slow notes, struck deliberately, with long rests between each strike. The motif completes only once, on Donggyu's waking. Diegetic sound is absolute and intimate. The wet click of porcelain. The small drag of water in metal. The dry scratch of mineral residue placed on skin. The viewer should be uncomfortable with how quiet it is.

**Color script**

| Shots | Palette |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Pale fluorescent surface. Sodium-warm. Hospital-white. |
| 4–7 | Gradient descent. White → gray → iron-brown → lampblack. |
| 8–14 | Lampblack with localized phosphor-soft Flushed light. |
| 15–20 | Lampblack with pale drain-light from above. |
| 21–22 | Pure black. Title. |

**Camera grammar**

The camera is largely static. When it moves, it moves slowly, on a vertical or horizontal axis only. No handheld. No dolly. No zoom. The film respects its subject. Hard cuts are preferred to dissolves; a single dissolve is permitted, in Shot 13, to render time-lapse.

## 00:00 — 00:12  ·  *Prologue: Surface*

### SHOT 1 — Exterior, dusk · 4 seconds
Wide static. Exterior of a five-story tenement at dusk. Most windows dark. One fifth-floor bathroom window glows. Powerlines cross the sky. A kite caught in them does not move.

> *Audio:* A distant car horn, faint. Otherwise silence.

### SHOT 2 — Interior, bathroom · 4 seconds
The fifth-floor bathroom. Tile floor. Wide static, camera at floor height. We see a man's bare feet, lying on the tile, one foot canted slightly outward. We do not see the rest of him. The flush handle is at the frame's upper right.

> *Audio:* A slow breath in. A slow breath out. Not in again.

### SHOT 3 — The ring · 4 seconds
Close on the man's hand. The hand rests on the toilet bowl's rim. It is bloodless. The wedding ring is loose. It slips, slowly, off the finger, and falls into the bowl, and is gone.

> *Audio:* The small ceramic *ting* of the ring on porcelain. Then the soft swallow of water. Nothing else.

---

## 00:12 — 00:24  ·  *The Fall*

### SHOT 4 — The handle · 3 seconds
Close on the handle. A second hand presses it. We do not yet see whose. The handle gives. The flush begins.

> *Audio:* The sound of a domestic flush, mixed reverently. A mechanism completing.

### SHOT 5 — Down the bowl · 3 seconds
High angle, looking straight down into the bowl. The man's body curls in the water, weightless, arms drawn close. He does not struggle. The water claims him.

> *Audio:* Only water.

### SHOT 6 — The pipes · 4 seconds
Black frame. From the top, a small pale form falls through the dark, slowly, trailing fiber-particulate. The descent is silent now.

> *Audio:* The piano strikes its first note. Hold.

### SHOT 7 — Closer · 2 seconds
Closer on the falling form. The body is no longer recognizable as the man on the tile floor. The clothes are sodden. The face is turned downward. Hair drifts upward in the negative gravity of free fall.

> *Audio:* The piano strikes its second note.

---

## 00:24 — 00:33  ·  *The Arrival*

### SHOT 8 — The landing · 3 seconds
The body lands. Lampblack. No splash. Only a soft, accepted thud onto something yielding. A bed of softened pulp. The body settles.

> *Audio:* The piano strikes its third note. Hold. The motif begins to repeat.

### SHOT 9 — The chamber reveals itself · 3 seconds
Wide static. The lampblack reveals itself slowly: a vault of pipe-vaulting overhead, hairline cracks of overhead-grate light, the contour of an enormous chamber. The body is small at the bottom-center of frame.

> *Audio:* Silence beneath the motif.

### SHOT 10 — The eyes · 3 seconds
Stillness held one beat too long. A single small phosphor light appears in the upper-left middle distance. Then a second, in the lower-right. Then six more, scattered. The viewer realizes they are eyes.

---

## 00:33 — 00:55  ·  *The Receiving*

### SHOT 11 — The first acolyte · 4 seconds
Medium shot. From the dark, a single Flushed approaches the body. It is a small thing, robed in softened paper, the silhouette of an acolyte. It stops at the body's feet. It kneels. It does not touch.

### SHOT 12 — The first residue · 5 seconds
Other Flushed arrive. They form a loose ring around the body. One, a Wipe-elder by its hood, produces from inside its robes a small pinch of mineral residue, white as bone-meal. It approaches the body's brow. It places the residue carefully, in a single deliberate line, down the center of the forehead.

> *Audio:* The dry, intimate scratch of grit on skin.

### SHOT 13 — Time-lapse, the calcification · 5 seconds
The camera holds. *A single permitted dissolve.* The body, motionless, accretes. Pale residue settles in the hollows of the collarbones, the wrists, the dip beneath the throat. It thickens. It hardens. It begins to look like armor.

> *Audio:* The piano motif continues, three notes, repeating, slow.

### SHOT 14 — The lens-offering · 4 seconds
Closer. A Lens-acolyte arrives last, bearing a single intact contact lens on a small ceramic dish. It does not place the lens on the body. It places the dish on the floor beside the body, as one places an offering. It kneels.

### SHOT 15 — The widest shot · 4 seconds
The widest shot in the film. We see the chamber entire: the ribbed pipe-vaulting overhead, the assembled congregation of the Flushed in a perfect ring, the body at the center now armored in calcified pale. Above, far above, through a small grate, a single fragment of overhead-grate light falls in a column. It does not reach the body.

> *Audio:* The piano motif pauses on its second note. Holds.

---

## 00:55 — 01:07  ·  *The Waking*

### SHOT 16 — The masked face · 3 seconds
Close on the masked face. The mineral has formed a smooth pale plate where the features were. There is no mouth. The eyes are sealed.

> *Audio:* Stillness held one beat too long.

### SHOT 17 — The eyes open · 3 seconds
The eyes open. Two pale points of light, mild and exhausted. The congregation does not gasp. The congregation bows.

> *Audio:* The piano strikes its third note. The motif completes for the first time.

### SHOT 18 — The pipe is offered · 3 seconds
From a Flushed acolyte's small hands, a sharpened length of copper pipe is presented, horizontally, across two palms. The metal catches the only warm light in the chamber.

### SHOT 19 — The pipe is taken · 3 seconds
Donggyu's hand closes around the pipe. Pale. Calcified. The acolyte withdraws.

---

## 01:07 — 01:15  ·  *The Look Up*

### SHOT 20 — He stands · 3 seconds
Donggyu stands. He is not steady. He does not need to be. He has been waited for.

### SHOT 21 — The vertical · 5 seconds
Extreme upward shot. From Donggyu's vantage, looking up the central shaft of the Pipework. Five stories. The grate at the top is so distant it reads as a single faint star. Drain-light falls along the long axis of the climb. Particulate drifts down past him, the way ash drifts past a survivor.

> *Audio:* The piano begins its motif again, from the first note.

---

## 01:15 — 01:18  ·  *Title*

### SHOT 22 — Title card · 3 seconds
Cut to black.

Title fades in, centered, in a serifed roman, small and weathered:

> **BACKFLOW**
>
> *…for those who were flushed.*

Hold three seconds. Cut hard to gameplay or main menu.

## Appendix — Performance Notes

- **The Flushed congregation** must be performed by puppeteers, not animators. Each individual Flushed should hold absolutely still between motions. They are not nervous. They are reverent. Reverence reads on screen as economy.
- **The Wipe-elder's anointing of the brow** in Shot 12 is the cinematic's emotional center. Three seconds of preparation, one second of contact, one second of withdrawal. Do not rush. Do not extend.
- **The viewer's first sight of Donggyu's open eyes** in Shot 17 must coincide with the piano's third note, struck exactly on frame. The cut to that shot defines the film's musical arc.
- **The kite in Shot 1** is the only image in the cinematic from the surface world. It must read as caught, not flying. The surface is also stuck. The descent is not a tragedy. It is a transfer.

The film has no dialogue because the world it depicts has not yet allowed Donggyu to speak. It will not allow him to speak in the game either. He will be silent for the entire campaign. The cinematic is, in this sense, a contract.
